# SARizard wiki

An Obsidian vault mapping the fit-to-purpose foundation study as a graph: which descriptor
target a CheMeleon-style foundation is pretrained on, and which ADMET endpoints each one
serves best. The two artifact nodes ([[Report Card]] and [[Meta-Model]]) are the center;
flavor nodes branch off [[Shared Corpus and Regime]], and each flavor links to the endpoint
families it is hypothesized to serve.

## Open it

Open this `wiki/` folder as an Obsidian vault (Open folder as vault), then open Graph view
(Ctrl/Cmd-G). The color groups in `.obsidian/graph.json` load automatically. Every page also
renders as plain Markdown in any viewer; the `[[ ]]` wikilinks are the graph edges. `_index`
and `README` are filtered out of the graph.

## Color legend

Status is carried in each note's `tags`. Until a flavor is finetuned and evaluated it is
planned (purple); once results land, recolor it by its verdict against the stock-CheMeleon
baseline. The verdict is Dunnett's test on the 5 finetune seeds of each flavor's mean R² across
the 32 endpoint-columns, at p ≤ 0.05, under the frozen and reduced protocols (unlocked is
excluded: it washes every flavor out, so it separates nothing). The 15 flavors within a protocol
are corrected together as one comparison family, since they all share the one stock control; an
uncorrected per-flavor t-test called six more flavors significant than survive that correction.

| Color | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟠 orange | Reference backbone (stock CheMeleon), not one of our flavors |
| 🔵 blue | Method, control, or endpoint-family node |
| 🟣 purple | Planned flavor (not yet pretrained, finetuned, or evaluated) |
| 🟢 green | Significantly above the stock baseline under frozen or reduced |
| 🟡 yellow | No significant difference from stock under either |
| 🔴 red | Significantly below stock under frozen or reduced |

As of the full-corpus 5-seed sweep no flavor is purple: every registry flavor has results.

## Source of record

Headline results live in the repo-root `FINDINGS.md`; running avenues in `TODO.md`. These
pages add per-flavor detail and the relations between flavors and endpoints. Where they
disagree, `FINDINGS.md` wins. Figures generated by `sarizard/analysis/report_card.py` and
`sarizard/analysis/meta_model.py` land in `plots/`; copy the ones worth narrating into
`figures/` and embed them.
